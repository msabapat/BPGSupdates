Indiv Stock Options Call Spread Strategy

Two overlay strategies deploy a fixed dollar amount per 10Δ/5Δ call-spread structure each month (up to 20 structures/month, capped by candidate availability), with all remaining capital held in SPY. 2-month hold: $1,000/structure, ~2 cohorts open concurrently. 1-month hold: $2,000/structure (2× the backtested $1,000 sizing, scaled linearly), 1 cohort open at a time. Option positions are tracked at cost between open and close (no interim daily mark-to-market -- only entry cost and terminal P&L are available from the backtest). Any capital not currently tied up in open structures -- including proceeds and P&L released when a cohort closes -- sits in SPY, and SPY is tracked via its dividend- and split-adjusted close (Sharadar closeadj), i.e. dividends are reinvested.
