Takeaway: Haiku 4.5 (low effort) delivers by far the best quality-per-dollar (550 index) for coding tasks, at roughly 1/5th to 1/50th the cost of the other models at their respective effort tiers. Sonnet 5 medium is the best value among the higher-quality tier (index ~94), noticeably ahead of Opus variants which pay a steep cost premium (8.1–8.5×) for comparable or modestly better quality (+11 to +13 pts). Pushing any model to max effort collapses value sharply — the marginal quality gain rarely justifies the 5–10× cost jump from medium to max.
