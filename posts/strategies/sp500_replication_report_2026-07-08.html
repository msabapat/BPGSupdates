Quality-500: Cap-Weighted Replication vs SPY

Monthly rebalance, 1999-01 → 2026-06 · Benchmark: SPY

Methodology

A mechanical, cap-weighted "quality S&P 500" replication, built monthly and compared against SPY over the same window:

  1. Start with the top 600 domestic common stocks by market cap (marketcap_ffill) as of each month-end.
  2. Filter out any stock with trailing-twelve-month net income < 0 (net losers excluded; breakeven or better stays in).
  3. Filter out any stock with less than 12 months of trading history as of that month-end (avoids newly-listed/IPO noise).
  4. Re-rank the survivors by market cap and take the top 500.
  5. Weight each holding by its market cap at the start of the month (fixed for the month — no intra-month drift rebalancing).
  6. Hold for one month; portfolio return = sum of (weight × stock return) using each stock's month-end-to-month-end price change. Repeat the next month with a freshly reconstructed universe and fresh market-cap weights.

No sector caps, no float-adjustment, no index-committee inclusion judgment — just a market-cap threshold plus a profitability and seasoning filter, rebuilt fresh every month.
