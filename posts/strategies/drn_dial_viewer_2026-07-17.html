dd (Drawdown %) = (NAV / running all-time-high − 1) × 100. Always ≤ 0. Resets to 0 whenever SPY makes a new high; otherwise tracks how far below that peak price sits today. Shown in the red panel below.
du (Drawup %) = (NAV / running trough-since-last-ATH − 1) × 100. Always ≥ 0. Tracks how far NAV has bounced off the lowest point reached since the last all-time high. Shown in the green panel below.
recovered_frac = (log NAV − log Trough) / (log ATH-at-cycle-start − log Trough). A fixed-anchor, log-scale measure of how much of THIS cycle's total trough-to-old-ATH gap has closed — 0 right at the trough, 1 once NAV regains the old high. Unlike the old formula's du ≥ |dd|×f test (which divides by the CURRENT, shrinking drawdown and so self-reinforces as price approaches the old high), this anchors the denominator once, at the trough, so the same fraction means the same thing whether the round-trip takes 2 days or 5 years.
T — minimum drawdown depth (%) required before a day can be Descent or Recovery at all; shallower dips stay Normal.
f_lo — the recovered_frac cutoff: below it while dd<−T is Descent, at/above it while still dd<−T is Recovery.
lag — causal reporting lag in trading days: today's zone label uses dd/ recovered_frac from lag days ago, so a trade acting on the label never uses same-day (unknowable-in-advance) information.
Old (locked) formula — Strategies/regime_config.py, currently in production, unchanged by any of this: Descent: dd<−T AND du<|dd|×f; Recovery: dd<−T×f AND du≥|dd|×f; Normal: else; locked T=12, f=0.9, causal lag 1 day. This is the formula the "Locked (old)" preset reproduces exactly, using the SAME T/f/lag dials (just reinterpreted per the old rule) so you can flip between old and new with everything else held constant.
