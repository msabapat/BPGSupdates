How FQW and FQWL are constructed

Both run through fundamental_signal_strategy.py, metric_col="composite". Universe: prefilter domestic_mcap_rank≤2000 (no ADRs/SPACs) + standard hard liquidity/mcap/revenue screens + mktcap_to_rev filter (per-date MAD-screened, keep ≤ that day's median). Composite score: three raw signals (fcf_yield_ttm, ep_ttm, interest_coverage), each MAD-screened (k=5) then converted to a per-date cross-sectional percentile rank (0–1), combined as a weighted average 0.5×fcf_yield_ttm + 0.3×ep_ttm + 0.2×interest_coverage (needs ≥1 valid signal). Signal frequency monthly_last_fri, daily trade, hysteresis buffer=2, equal-weight, 5bps/side transaction costs.

FQW: sort all eligible survivors by the composite score descending, take the top N=25.
FQWL: two-stage. Stage 1 — sort by composite score descending, take the top 100 (vol_stage2_pool=100). Stage 2 — re-sort that pool of 100 by vol_60d ascending (lowest realized vol first), take the top 25 from that ordering. Same universe/screens/composite as FQW -- the only difference is this final low-vol re-rank.
