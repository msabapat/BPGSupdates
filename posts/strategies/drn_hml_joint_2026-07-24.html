Does combining HML with DRN improve the blended strategy?

Answer: no — the gain is a parameter-count artifact. BPGS · Blended Strategy · 2026-07-24
Verdict: overfitting. Keep DRN alone. The joint DRN×HML partition improves in-sample Sharpe (0.96 → 1.04) and gives it back out-of-sample (1.03 → 0.99). A randomly time-shifted HML series beats the DRN baseline in-sample in 100% of 200 placebo draws — any subdivision produces the in-sample gain. The real HML lands at the 26th percentile of that placebo distribution in-sample and the 27th out-of-sample: it is a below-median way to subdivide DRN, not an informative one. Under walk-forward refitting, DRN alone wins outright (Sharpe 0.97 vs 0.83).
Regime definitions & study parameters

DRN (Descent / Recovery / Normal) — canonical Config C, locked 2026-07-17, from regime_config.py. NAV = SPY total-return index; DD=(NAV/ATH−1)×100, DU=(NAV/Trough−1)×100.

recovered_frac = log(1+DU/100) / [log(1+DU/100) − log(1+DD/100)]
Descent:  DD < −12%  AND  recovered_frac < 0.5
Recovery: DD < −12%  AND  recovered_frac ≥ 0.5
Normal:   otherwise
Causal lag: 2 trading days

HML (High / Middle / Low) — trailing 3yr SPY return, cut at the 25% / 75% percentiles of a rolling 756-day history, lagged 1 day.

Allocation method — M1 fractional Kelly, as in kelly_blend.py: per box, IS tangency Sharpe over the 4 risky sleeves (60% cap each) → Kelly vol target σ = f × tangency_Sharpe → max-expected-return weights subject to that vol. Headline f = 0.25 (locked). M1 rather than M4 vol-target because M4's BASE_VOL constants are DRN-specific with no HML analogue; Kelly derives its target from each box's own tangency Sharpe and so extends to any partition without inventing new constants.

Components — MR (C150/X10), MOM (N500), LV (N300), SPY, T-bills, from output/blend/returns_panel.csv. Sharpe/Sortino are T-bill excess. IS 2001-01-26 → 2021-12-31 (5,267 days), OOS 2022-01-01 → 2026-06-12 (1,115 days).

Min-cell rule — a joint cell with fewer than 100 IS days does not get its own weight vector; it inherits its DRN-marginal weights. Without this the 3-day Descent×High cell would be independently optimized. The rule is applied identically to the placebos.

What this does and doesn't say

It says the discrete 3×3 cross of these two specific labelings does not improve the blend, and that the in-sample gain from doing so is indistinguishable from what any arbitrary subdivision produces. DRN alone stays the locked regime scheme for M1/M4.

It does not say HML is worthless as a descriptive lens — it remains useful for regime breakdown reporting (the standing 7-period convention), where the question is "how did this strategy do in high/middle/low markets," not "what should I allocate." Nor does it rule out a continuous regime coordinate: the failure here is specifically that crossing two discrete labelings multiplies parameters faster than it adds information, which is the same argument already motivating the continuous-regime research thread.
